About Me

Hello, My name is Olukolade Kaka, I'm a front-end web developer with a love for minimalism, clean design, and smooth animations. I build beautiful, accessible websites for small businesses and personal projects.

Beyond coding, I have experience working as an Etsy assistant, supporting store owners in managing and growing their online shops. My role involves helping create and organize product listings, preparing visuals, and ensuring products are presented clearly and professionally to attract customers.

Through this experience, I have developed strong attention to detail and a better understanding of how digital marketplaces operate. It has also helped me combine creativity with technical skills while collaborating with clients to turn their product ideas into well-structured listings ready for sale.
